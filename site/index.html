05 / BOUNDARIES

What this does and does not promise.

Does it guarantee sales?

No. It does not guarantee revenue, ROAS, legal compliance, platform acceptance, virality, perfect visual consistency, or any business outcome.

What do I need to provide?

Product facts and prohibited claims, audience, offer and price, brand voice, owned or licensed images, destination channel, campaign goal, known proof, visual constraints, and any known legal or platform restrictions.

Can I use it for client delivery?

Yes, with the Agency license. You may use the workflow in client delivery, but you may not resell or redistribute the underlying library.

What is in the free sample?

The fact-lock and creative-claim release gate, two owned fixture examples, and a deterministic verifier. Buyer strategy, concept generation, production briefs, test planning, and learning stages stay in the paid library.

Which AI tools do I need?

You need access to a capable text model. Image and video models are only required when you choose to produce those assets. Exact packaging or label reproduction still requires human inspection.
