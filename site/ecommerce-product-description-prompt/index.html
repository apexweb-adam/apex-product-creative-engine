Ecommerce product description prompt: write persuasive copy without inventing claims.

A reliable AI product description prompt does three jobs separately: it locks approved evidence, controls the copy structure, and audits every factual claim before publication. DIRECT ANSWER

What is an ecommerce product description prompt?

An ecommerce product description prompt is a structured instruction that tells an AI model which product evidence it may use, what copy it must produce, which claims are prohibited, and how to audit the result. The safest version treats unsupported facts as errors to repair or block, not gaps the model may fill.

A copy prompt controls expression

It can specify audience, brand voice, channel, length, title, benefits, feature order, usage guidance, caveats, and call to action.

A fact lock controls evidence

It separates verified facts from derived wording, unsupported claims, and conflicts. Every factual sentence must be traceable to an approved source.

A release gate controls publication

It produces a fixed PASS, REPAIR, or BLOCK decision and preserves unresolved issues for human review.

01 / FIVE-STEP WORKFLOW

How to use AI for product descriptions without letting it guess.

The workflow below keeps factual product claims separate from creative choices. Each step should produce evidence a reviewer can inspect.

  1. 01

    Lock the approved evidence

    Provide numbered source blocks for product identity, materials, dimensions, compatibility, included items, care instructions, current price, offer terms, and approved proof. Add prohibited claims and known restrictions. If a source is missing, say that it is missing.

  2. 02

    Separate facts from creative choices

    Allow changes to tone, order, headings, formatting, and calls to action. Require a source reference for product behavior, performance, safety, certification, review, popularity, comparison, price, scarcity, or guaranteed-result claims.

  3. 03

    Draft to a fixed output contract

    Define the exact deliverable before generation. A useful contract might require a product title, short summary, three benefits, feature details, usage guidance, limitations, call to action, and a list of evidence gaps.

  4. 04

    Audit every claim

    Classify each factual statement as VERIFIED, DERIVED, UNSUPPORTED, or CONFLICT. Remove or safely rewrite unsupported wording. Block high-risk claims that need evidence or specialist review.

  5. 05

    Release only after human review

    Check the final copy together with the price, offer, images, rights, product identity, safety context, regulated claims, and channel rules. The reviewer, not the model, owns the publication decision.

Why evidence control matters

The United States Federal Trade Commission states that advertising claims must be truthful, not deceptive or unfair, and evidence-based. ROLE
You are an ecommerce product copywriter and claim auditor.

APPROVED SOURCES
[1] Product identity and specifications: ...
[2] Included items and usage guidance: ...
[3] Current price and offer: ...

PROHIBITED CLAIMS
Do not invent performance, safety, certification, reviews,
popularity, discounts, scarcity, comparisons, or buyer results.

TASK
Draft a product title, short summary, three benefits,
feature details, usage guidance, limitations, and CTA.

CLAIM RULE
Map every factual claim to a numbered source.
Mark missing support as UNSUPPORTED. Never guess.

RELEASE OUTPUT
Return the draft, claim audit, safe repairs,
missing evidence, do-not-generate list, and one
PASS, REPAIR, or BLOCK decision.

06 / QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions.

What is the best prompt for ecommerce product descriptions?

A reliable prompt separates approved evidence from creative instructions, requires source mapping for factual claims, and ends with a fixed PASS, REPAIR, or BLOCK decision before publication.

How do I stop AI from inventing product features?

Give the model numbered sources, prohibit unsupported claims, require a claim-by-claim audit, and move missing or conflicting facts into a human review queue instead of letting the model guess.

Can AI-generated product descriptions be published without review?

They should not be treated as release-ready without human review. Product identity, price, offer, rights, safety, regulated claims, images, and channel rules still require an accountable human decision.

Does fact locking guarantee legal compliance or sales?

No. Fact locking helps control unsupported claims, but it does not guarantee legal compliance, marketplace approval, conversion, revenue, or return on ad spend.
